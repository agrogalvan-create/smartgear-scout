About SmartGear Scout

SmartGear Scout is an editorial pilot focused on helping readers understand technical specifications when comparing pumps, irrigation equipment and home tools. Our content is informational, not a substitute for qualified installation advice. We distinguish research from hands-on testing and do not claim to have tested products unless we have.

How we research

We start with the problem a reader needs to solve, then identify measurable selection criteria such as operating pressure, flow rate, electrical compatibility, safety requirements and maintenance needs. Where we describe specific products, manufacturer documentation is a starting point rather than proof of real-world performance.

What we do not claim

We do not claim first-hand testing, independent laboratory results, live prices, product availability or endorsements unless expressly supported. We do not currently display active affiliate offers.

Corrections and updates

Specifications and product availability can change. Readers should check current manufacturer documentation and applicable local requirements before buying or installing equipment. We review guides for accuracy when new information becomes available.
