How to choose

Rachio 3

Consider when app-based scheduling is a priority and the controller can be mounted indoors. Rachio states its app has no monthly fees. Outdoor installations require an additional enclosure.

Orbit B-hyve XR

Consider when you want an indoor/outdoor unit with dual-band Wi-Fi and built-in local status displays. Confirm the exact 8- or 16-station model before ordering.

Hunter X2 with WAND

Consider when physical dial-and-button programming and optional online control are important. The WAND module is sold separately: budget for both components if Wi-Fi control is required.

Installation checklist

  • Count active irrigation zones and allow for planned expansion.
  • Confirm 24 VAC valve and pump-start relay compatibility.
  • Check Wi-Fi coverage at the mounting location, especially 2.4 GHz support.
  • Confirm outdoor enclosure requirements and electrical protection.
  • Verify rain/freeze sensor compatibility and manual fallback.
  • Review local water utility rebates and their model-specific eligibility.
