Smart Sprinkler Controller Buying Checklist

Before comparing prices, check whether a controller will actually work with your irrigation system. This independent checklist is based on general installation principles, not hands-on product testing.

Six checks before ordering

  1. Zone count: Count connected valve stations and leave room for expansion.
  2. Valve compatibility: Confirm voltage and wiring, including master valve or pump-start relay.
  3. Wi-Fi: Verify signal strength and supported bands where the controller will be installed.
  4. Mounting: Check indoor/outdoor rating, weatherproof enclosure and electrical requirements.
  5. Controls: Decide whether you need local programming if internet access fails.
  6. Total cost: Include any separate modules, enclosure, accessories and optional paid features.
