Sprinkler Zone Not Working: Troubleshooting Checklist

Independent technical guide · November 2026. General educational information, not field testing or a site-specific engineering assessment.

Identify the symptom

Does the zone never activate, run only manually, operate intermittently or deliver weak flow? These patterns point to different causes. Check whether other zones work normally before assuming the controller has failed.

Controller and schedule checks

Confirm the program is enabled, the correct start times and station runtimes are set, and rain-delay or seasonal settings are not preventing operation. Inspect sensor status and follow the controller's documented manual-test procedure.

Water supply and valve checks

Verify that the irrigation supply is available and the applicable isolation valves are in the intended position. A valve that fails to open may involve electrical controls, a solenoid or mechanical obstruction. Do not disassemble pressurized components.

Electrical safety

Turn off power before inspecting wiring. Outdoor wiring, transformers and valve circuits should be assessed according to manufacturer instructions and applicable electrical rules. Have a qualified technician diagnose damaged cables, uncertain voltage or exposed conductors.

Weak flow versus no flow

If heads rise only partly, check for leaks, pressure loss, clogged components or excessive zone demand. Use the pressure and flow checklist before changing pump or nozzle sizes.

When to call for service

Stop if there is water near electrical equipment, repeated breaker trips, unexplained pressure loss or an inaccessible buried fault. Record zone number, symptoms, controller model and recent changes for the technician.
