How to choose a sump pump and plan a backup

Independent editorial checklist · November 2026. No hands-on product testing or affiliate links are claimed.

A sump pump reduces certain flooding risks but cannot guarantee protection from all storms, groundwater conditions, sewer backups or power outages.

Measure the lift and discharge route

Measure the vertical rise from the pump to the discharge point, then account for pipe length, fittings and check-valve losses. Compare candidate pump curves at the resulting operating head. A pump's advertised maximum flow is usually measured under favorable conditions and is not the flow delivered at every lift.

Estimate inflow, not just basin volume

Review historical peak inflow during heavy rain or snowmelt. A sump basin fills at different rates depending on soil, drainage design and local groundwater. Where flooding consequences are high, consult a qualified drainage professional to estimate design inflow.

Check switch operation and basin clearance

Float switches need room to move without snagging pipes, cables or basin walls. Confirm the manufacturer's minimum basin diameter and required water levels. Test the switch using the manufacturer's instructions; do not reach into energized equipment.

Consider power outages and failure modes

A battery backup or secondary pump may reduce risk during an outage or primary-pump failure, but batteries have limited runtime and require inspection and replacement. Evaluate alarm options, check valves and a safe discharge route that does not return water toward the foundation.

Installation and maintenance checks

  • Use an appropriately protected electrical outlet and follow local electrical code.
  • Verify the discharge arrangement complies with local drainage and sewer rules.
  • Keep the pit covered where required and accessible for inspection.
  • Test the system periodically and before expected wet seasons.
  • Check for frozen or obstructed discharge piping in cold climates.

Questions to ask before purchasing

Request the full pump performance curve, rated voltage, maximum solids handling (if relevant), switch type, recommended basin dimensions, warranty, service availability and backup-system compatibility. Match the product to the actual application rather than choosing by horsepower alone.
