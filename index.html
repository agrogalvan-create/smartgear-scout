What matters when choosing equipment?

Well pumps

Estimate required flow and total dynamic head, then check pump curves and well yield. Horsepower alone is not a selection criterion.

Smart irrigation controllers

Check valve compatibility, number of zones, weather adjustments, Wi-Fi requirements and manual override.

Drip irrigation

Design around flow, filtration, pressure regulation, emitter spacing and maintenance.

Booster pumps

Match operating pressure and simultaneous flow demand; account for supply limitations and local codes.

Sump pumps

Compare pumping capacity at the actual lift, discharge sizing, float reliability and backup options.

Pressure washers

Evaluate both pressure (PSI) and water flow (GPM), plus nozzle suitability and surface safety.
