# Amazon US affiliate eligibility and payout research — 2026-11-17

Official sources:
- https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies
- https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GGD9H76RMDDNEWAE
- https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G8VUMS6GTBCR9RGV

## Verified
- Amazon US Associates is free to join.
- Application requires a public website with substantive original content; Amazon reviews applications and may reject unsuitable sites.
- Its policy explicitly discusses non-US participants, so non-US residence alone is not stated as an automatic prohibition. **This does not confirm approval for our operator in Argentina.**
- Commission payout normally occurs about 60 days after the calendar month in which commissions are earned.
- Published US payment minimums: $10 direct deposit, $10 gift card, $100 check; checks can incur a $15 processing fee, subject to international exceptions.
- Bank transfer availability depends on the bank country and the account/payment configuration. **An Argentine bank transfer route has not been verified.**
- Amazon price/availability display is restricted: use its own approved linking tools or compliant API data; do not copy changing prices manually.
- Automated agents interacting with Amazon program content must comply with the policy's agent identification and access requirements. No automated Amazon scraping should be implemented.

## Still blocked
- Confirm exact applicant eligibility, bank details and tax interview for the actual account.
- Confirm a production host with terms permitting a commercial affiliate website.
- Do not apply, disclose banking details or publish affiliate links until the user approves those actions.

## Launch sequence
1. Finish editorial pages and site link checks.
2. Select permitted hosting.
3. Publish the original content.
4. Apply to the affiliate program after user approval.
5. Add approved links and measure actual results.
